FindYour4th logo kit  v1
========================
svg/   master vector files (all text converted to outlines; no fonts needed)
png/   raster exports (transparent unless the name says on-green)

Primary:    svg/findyour4th-logo.svg  (mark green, "FindYour" ink, "4th" green)
One colour: findyour4th-logo-green.svg / -ink.svg
Reversed:   findyour4th-logo-on-green.svg (canvas #F6F5F2 on green #0F5132)
Mark only:  findyour4th-mark.svg  (use under ~120 px wide, or as an avatar)
App icons:  findyour4th-app-icon.svg (square, full-bleed, maskable-safe)
            findyour4th-app-icon-rounded.svg (web)
Favicon:    findyour4th-favicon.svg, png/favicon.ico, png/apple-touch-icon.png
            (favicons use the small-size mark: wider gaps, heavier ring)

Colours: Green #0F5132 | Ink #1A1A1A | Canvas #F6F5F2
Clear space: keep at least one dot-diameter free on every side.
Minimum size: mark 16 px, full lockup 96 px wide.
Wordmark: Sora SemiBold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines.
Don't: add gradients/shadows, recolour the open seat, stretch, or rotate.
